# Terrain integration concept targets

These images were generated with OpenAI Image Gen as implementation references for
the Blender/Three.js scenes. They are not runtime skyboxes, billboards, or substitutes
for modeled geometry. Their purpose is to make terrain continuity, scale, material,
waterline, and silhouette decisions testable during visual QA.

## Urithiru

Output: `terrain-integration-urithiru-target.png`

References:

- The supplied Urithiru blueprint and east/west section study.
- The two supplied Urithiru concept paintings.
- The current in-engine Urithiru QA screenshot.

Prompt:

> Create one photorealistic, implementation-oriented visual development image for a
> production Blender and Three.js version of Urithiru. Preserve the supplied blueprint's
> enormous stepped, rounded tower and mountain-cut silhouette, but show it physically
> excavated into one continuous mountain rather than sitting on a pedestal. The lower
> tiers, gates, forecourt, retaining walls, roads, snow shelves, drainage, rock strata,
> talus, and inhabited terraces must merge continuously into an asymmetric mountain
> shoulder. Include tiny people, carts, bridges, windows, Windrunners in flight, and
> readable entrances for scale. Use weathered PBR stone, metal, ice, snow, crem, warm
> inhabited windows, storm light, atmospheric depth, and modular forms feasible in
> Blender and Three.js. No floating city, dark fan-shaped base, detached mountain props,
> matte-painting replacement, text, UI, or presentation plinth.

Prompt:

> Create a single wide, three-panel implementation-oriented visual development board
