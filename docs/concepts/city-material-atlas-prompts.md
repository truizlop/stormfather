# City material atlas prompts

Mode: built-in ImageGen, project-bound generation. Every result is a square
surface sheet for genuine modeled geometry, never a complete city image or
camera-facing relief.

The generated sources were resized to 1024×1024, stripped of metadata, and
edge-matched before being saved under `public/textures/cities/`. The runtime
mapping contract and physical repeat sizes are recorded in that directory's
`manifest.json`.

## Alethi / Kholinar

> Use case: photorealistic-natural  
> Asset type: seamless PBR albedo material sheet for a real-time Three.js /
> Blender city architecture kit  
> Primary request: create one square tileable Alethi and Kholinar architectural
> material atlas, dominated by storm-weathered warm fortress stone with a
> smaller integrated band of very dark aged timber; both materials should feel
> massive, defensive, practical, and repeatedly battered by highstorms  
> Style/medium: ultra-detailed photogrammetry-quality physically based game
> material, true surface scan realism, no stylization  
> Composition/framing: strict orthographic straight-on 1:1 surface sample,
> edge-to-edge material only, no perspective, no horizon, no floor, no separate
> objects; arrange broad warm stone field with natural mortar courses and a
> compatible dark timber plank section so the sheet can be mapped to modeled
> walls and beams  
> Lighting/mood: perfectly diffuse neutral overcast illumination, no
> directional light, no cast shadows, no highlights, uniform exposure across
> all edges  
> Color palette: iron-rich russet, warm brown-grey, muted ochre crem deposits,
> charcoal-brown timber  
> Materials/textures: large fitted storm-fortress masonry, chipped corners,
> shallow impact scars, gritty mineral aggregate, hairline fractures,
> wind-driven rain streaks, pale crem packed in joints, subtle lichen only in
> recesses; dark dense timber with visible grain, peg holes, abrasion and
> repaired splits  
> Constraints: seamless and tileable on all four edges; physically plausible
> scale; high-frequency microdetail but low-frequency tonal balance; original
> material; no baked depth illusion; no architectural openings  
> Avoid: city scene, buildings, doors, windows, people, props, weapons, glyphs,
> symbols, text, borders, labels, watermark, dramatic shadows, vignette,
> perspective, repeated obvious motifs, fantasy ornament, mossy European castle
> look
