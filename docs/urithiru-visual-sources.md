# Urithiru visual sources

This brief separates directly attested visual facts from project interpretation.
The runtime must use original geometry, materials, and effects; official art is a
silhouette and worldbuilding reference, not a texture or model source.

## Canon facts

### Tower and mountain

- Urithiru is an ancient Knights Radiant tower-city nestled in mountains high
  above the storms:
  <https://www.brandonsanderson.com/pages/the-stormlight-archive-series>.
- Its exterior has ten ringlike tiers of eighteen stories each. Lower tiers are
  wider, with aqueducts, windows, balconies, garden or field space on tier roofs,
  and broad farm plateaus with furrows and planter boxes around the base:
  <https://reactormag.com/oathbringer-brandon-sanderson-chapter-1-3/>.
- The rings are semicircular rather than complete cylinders. Their flat sides
  face east; lower levels merge into the mountains on both sides, while a central
  opening and immense, continuous glass wall face east. The top platform is still
  more than one hundred yards wide:
  <https://reactormag.com/oathbringer-by-brandon-sanderson-chapters-4-6/>.
- Most of the structure is embedded in the mountain. Only near the upper tiers
  does the tower fully rise above the surrounding stone. The eighth-tier
  Cloudwalk is an open, railed garden path around almost the whole tier:
  <https://reactormag.com/read-rhythm-of-war-by-brandon-sanderson-chapter-sixteen/>.
- The city is carved from stone, with high ceilings and distinctive multicolored
  strata running through its surfaces:
  <https://reactormag.com/read-rhythm-of-war-by-brandon-sanderson-chapter-eighteen/>
  and
  <https://reactormag.com/oathbringer-by-brandon-sanderson-chapters-28-30/>.
- Ben McSweeney's in-book Shallan sketch is the most useful official silhouette
  reference:
  <https://coppermind.net/wiki/File:Urithiru.jpg>.

### Oathgates

- Ten Oathgates connect Urithiru to distant cities. People standing on a city's
  Oathgate platform are transported to the matching platform at Urithiru:
  <https://reactormag.com/oathbringer-brandon-sanderson-chapter-1-3/>.
- Therefore, an Oathgate name labels a **teleport destination**. It does not mean
  that the named city is physically located on, represented by, or assigned to
  one of Urithiru's terraces. UI labels must read as routes or destinations, not
  as districts of the tower.

### Windrunners around Urithiru

- Windrunners are protection-focused scouts and special-forces soldiers with a
  strong squad and command structure:
  <https://www.brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/the-ten-orders-of-knights-radiant>.
- In flight, blue-uniformed squads can hover motionless at even spacing, then
  swoop or bank. They can wield bright Shardspears that form from mist:
  <https://reactormag.com/read-rhythm-of-war-by-brandon-sanderson-chapters-four-and-five/>.
- Windrunners glow with Stormlight. Escaping or exhaled Light reads as luminous
  mist or smoke; the text does not establish rigid, persistent rocket contrails.
  Syl can appear blue-white with hair fading to mist, or as a ribbon of light:
  <https://reactormag.com/read-rhythm-of-war-by-brandon-sanderson-chapters-two-and-three/>.
- Isaac Stewart's Bridge Four shoulder patch belongs on a Kholin-blue uniform:
  <https://www.dragonsteelbooks.com/products/bridge-4-patch>.

## Implementation inferences

- Model Urithiru as a mountain reshaped into a vast terraced city, not as a
  freestanding cylindrical skyscraper. Bury the broad tier shoulders in jagged
  peaks and keep the upper tower visibly wider than an ordinary building.
- Use the flat east face, central glass wall, repeating balconies, terrace fields,
  and tiny airborne figures to make its otherwise difficult scale legible.
- Pale gray or tan layered stone with ice and snow on exposed upper surfaces is a
  safe project palette, but the exact exterior stone color is not fixed by the
  cited prose.
- Arrange five to nine Windrunners as a disciplined patrol: one readable
  spear-bearing leader, a few evenly hovering sentinels, and a few banking around
  the tiers. Include men and women.
- Give flyers restrained white or pale blue-white Stormlight shedding close to
  the body. Use only a few visible honorspren because many airborne Windrunners
  may be squires rather than independently bonded knights.
- Ordinary highstorms pass below Urithiru. A cloud sea or distant storm beneath
  the tower is appropriate; a normal highstorm engulfing or towering over the
  summit is not.
- Avoid angel wings, jetpacks, neon exhaust trails, a modern glass-skyscraper
  silhouette, a complete round wedding-cake form, dense lowland forest, or a tiny
  needle-like summit.

## Spoiler-conscious rules

1. The default atlas state is the early *Oathbringer* presentation: cold,
   austere, mostly unlit stone, sparse or dormant terrace growth, and no revealed
   tower-wide magical activation.
2. Windrunner patrols and Bridge Four identity are safe for that state, but large
   later-era forces should remain a separate chronology option.
3. Towerlight, lights glowing from the stone, fully restored systems, and abundant
   renewed plant life are post-*Rhythm of War* or later imagery. Do not enable
   them in the default scene or describe their cause in spoiler-light UI.
4. Treat Michael Whelan's *Wind and Truth* cover as an interpretive mood
   reference, not exact architectural canon, and keep it out of spoiler-light
   copy:
   <https://www.michaelwhelan.com/shop/wind-and-truth/>.
