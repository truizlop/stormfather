# Stormfather v2 reference ledger

Research and visual comparison: 5–6 September 2026. Source drawings take precedence over generated imagery. `docs/concepts/v2/field-atlas.png` was rejected for its invented geography: it is a HUD styling study only.

## Geographic constraints

[Isaac Stewart’s official Roshar map, Dragonsteel](https://www.dragonsteelbooks.com/products/roshar-map-poster) establishes the broad east–west continent, northern Reshi Sea and island arc, western Shinovar, inland Purelake, eastern Alethkar and the southern Thaylen island. The new renderer triangulates the existing registered coastline and lake polygons in `src/world/cartography/geography.generated.ts`. Refinement adds interior vertices without moving coastline vertices. Place anchors retain the same registration. Continental heights and biome shading are interpretive; this is not a surveyed digital elevation model. A north-up camera supports direct silhouette comparison.

Akinah’s continental marker represents **Aimia**, not a surveyed island location. Gazetteer records retain precise, regional or unknown confidence. Unknown entries receive no invented pin.

## Living world

[Chulls](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Chull): six legs, mineral shell, domestic cargo and retraction. [Axehounds](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Axehound): six limbs and antennae. [Chasmfiends](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Chasmfiend): eighteen limbs, four prominent foreclaws and a plated body. [Skyeels](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Skyeel): airborne eel form and fins, placed in coastal environments. The [author’s world introduction](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/pages/the-stormlight-archive-series) supports storm-adapted ecology.

The simulation uses original articulated geometry. Humans walk authored paths checked against building volumes, dwell at endpoints and seek covered endpoints during storms. It is an ambient illustration, not a demographic or full city traffic model. Smaller creature variations are artistic interpretations. The shared clock freezes travel, gait, water, rain and weather progression when paused. Ordinary storms pass below Urithiru and weaken in Shinovar.

## Retrieval and rights

Official map and city images were downloaded to temporary reference storage and visually inspected. Several Coppermind pages rejected direct requests; indexed text and accessible aliases supplied their book-cited descriptions. Reference artwork is linked, not repackaged as project-owned art or texture. All new exported geometry is original fan-made reconstruction. Sources may contain spoilers. The depiction is an illustrative intact world; it does not assert that every shown condition coexists at one exact moment in the books.

## Continuous atlas, palette review and Radiants — 6 September 2026

Cities now occupy the same coordinate system as the registered continent. Camera travel, scroll zoom, street cameras and actor follow all use one world transform. Local metres are enlarged at up to **0.002 atlas units per metre** for legibility; this is not a claim about the cities’ geographic area. Regional terrain joins replace the former square model bases, and continental relief tapers to the mapped shore without changing its horizontal outline. Akinah remains a representative reconstruction at the Aimia regional marker, not a newly asserted precise location.

Re-inspected the published Kharbranth port illustration, Ben McSweeney’s Urithiru sketch and the Great Thaylen City survey. These monochrome/sepia drawings establish form and urban relationships, **not a literal sepia building palette**. Reviewed the [author’s art-direction commentary](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/oathbringer-art-roundup-part-1) as context for distinguishing drawings and color paintings.

- **Kharbranth:** increased the red/orange share and saturation of painted façades, with blue/yellow accents, violet merchant façades and green food shops. This is grounded in the book-cited [architecture description](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Kharbranthian); exact RGB swatches and the assignment to individual reconstructed buildings are interpretations. Bright commercial façades do not establish an exact canon color for every home.
- **Urithiru:** retained pale grey stone and cream trim, with restrained earthy strata variation. The monochrome exterior sketch cannot establish a measured exterior palette. [Book-cited strata descriptions](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Urithiru?mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop) support earthy layers but also distinguish what Surgebinders can perceive; no claim that every surface is vividly multicolored.
- **Kholinar / Azimir:** retained the described layered windblade palette and differentiated warm bronze palace surfaces; no substitution of gold, blue glass or European slate roofs based on unrelated fantasy artwork.
- **Thaylen City / Vedenar / landscape dwellings:** retain restrained stone/earth materials. The available plans do not establish exact façade colors, so these remain explicitly interpreted.
- **Lighting:** daylight is more neutral so pale stone, blue cloth and red façades retain their material colors. Fine procedural texture is filtered at distance to reduce aliasing.

**Windrunners:** [Brandon Sanderson’s order description](https://www.brandonsanderson.com/blogs/blog/the-ten-orders-of-knights-radiant) describes protection, scouting, conventional military organization and squad training. The publisher’s [Rhythm of War sampler](https://www.hachette.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/Rhythm-of-War-UK-Chapter-sampler.pdf), PDF pages 58 and 68, supports blue uniforms, airborne formations, ordinary lances and visible leaking Stormlight. The in-book Urithiru illustration also includes a flying spear carrier. Twelve human-scale figures now patrol the open western approaches at several heights, wearing blue coats and carrying ordinary spears, with pale blue-white light wisps. Their routes and count are an illustrative activity, not a named chapter event; they are not labeled as specific characters or automatically given living Shardplate. The Radiants layer is independent of the civilian crowd, and the Urithiru Radiants camera follows one patrol. Other cities are not assigned permanent Radiant garrisons without a defensible temporal/location reference.

Order costumes use gemstone/placard accents; Stormlight particles remain pale blue-white. Only one Bondsmith representative appears.
