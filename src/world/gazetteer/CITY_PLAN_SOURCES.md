# Kholinar and Azimir city-plan sources

This supplement records internal-city points from two user-supplied renderings
of official Stormlight Archive maps. The image files are not copied into this
repository. Their pixels are retained only as auditable placement references.

## Placement policy

- A `placementReference.pixel` is precise **within its cited city plan**.
- Every global `referencePixel` is the parent city's existing continental
  anchor, so every entry is deliberately marked `regional`.
- `sourceMapPixel` remains `null`: city-plan pixels are not coordinates in the
  17th Shard 1024 × 512 continental source map.
- The plans have not been georeferenced, so these records must not be presented
  as separately surveyed world pins.

## Kholinar

- Supplied rendering: 858 × 1320 pixels.
- Official map: [Map of Kholinar](https://coppermind.net/wiki/File:Kholinar.jpg),
  an *Oathbringer* interior map by Isaac Stewart. Its file page transcribes the
  lettered places of interest and ten numbered Herald temples.
- Cross-checks:
  [Kholinar](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Kholinar),
  [Kholinar Palace](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Kholinar_Palace),
  [Vorinism](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Vorinism), and
  [Impossible Falls](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Impossible_Falls).

The catalog includes the nine readable requested places of interest:
Monastery Dais, Market Row, Dueling Arena, Theater Square, Sunmaker Park,
Lanacin Monument, Devotary of Insight, Impossible Falls, and Order of
Talenelat. It also includes the ten numbered temple labels: Jezerezeh, Nalan,
Chanaranach, Vedeledev, Pailiah, Shalash, Battah, Kelek, Talenelat, and Ishi.
Kholinar Palace and the Sunwalk are omitted here because the main catalog
already contains those records.

## Azimir

- Supplied rendering: 600 × 894 pixels.
- Official map: [Plan of the City of Azimir](https://coppermind.net/wiki/File:Plan_of_the_City_of_Azimir.jpg),
  a *Wind and Truth* interior map by Isaac Stewart.
- Cross-checks:
  [Azimir](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Azimir),
  [Bronze Palace](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Bronze_Palace),
  [Battle of Azimir](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Battle_of_Azimir), and
  [Thunderclast](https://coppermind.net/wiki/Thunderclast).

The catalog includes the five readable named features relevant to the supplied
plan: Bronze Palace, Grand Market, Azimir Hospital, Watchpost Tower, and Path
of the Thunderclast. The handwritten "was a nice pub" annotation is not a
proper name, and the parenthetical comment about the thunderclast is an event
note rather than another place; neither is promoted into the gazetteer.
